Legit Remote Job Opportunities

Sorted by highest pay first. Focus: remote-friendly roles with no occupational license required, or only simple optional certifications. Updated May 24, 2026.

Apply first if you want the best pay without a license: QA tester, project coordinator, SaaS sales/BDR, web developer, technical writer, marketing/data analyst, executive assistant, and IT support.

Scam filter: Apply only through the official company career page or a vetted job board. Do not pay for a job, training, background check, software, equipment, or "starter kit." Do not deposit a check to buy equipment. Ignore recruiters who move everything to WhatsApp, Telegram, or text and offer a job without a real interview. See the FTC remote job scam warning.

Skip or Be Extra Careful With These

  • "Task" jobs that make you deposit money to get paid: skip immediately.
  • Remote data entry promising very high pay: usually scam-heavy unless it is on an official employer site.
  • Reshipping/package forwarding jobs: high scam and legal risk.
  • Crypto, gift card, or payment-processing jobs: skip.
  • Commission-only insurance/sales jobs that make you buy leads: not worth it unless you fully understand the license, pay structure, and company.

Fast Verification Checklist

  • Find the job again on the company's official careers page before applying.
  • Check the recruiter email domain. It should match the real company domain, not Gmail, Outlook, Telegram, or a lookalike domain.
  • Expect a real interview by phone/video. Text-only hiring is a major red flag.
  • Never pay upfront or send money back after receiving a check.
  • Do not provide SSN, bank details, or tax forms until after a verified offer through the company's official HR system.
