About PSE

A public-facing commission sales operation, kept intentionally simple.

Pilot Sales Enterprise recruits and coordinates remote contractors for websites, compliant health insurance lead or appointment generation, barnacle removal, solar panel bird proofing, lead generation, and follow-up support. The public site gives applicants the essentials without exposing private operating details.

What We Do

We organize sales work into clear roles.

The goal is to make each opportunity understandable before someone applies: what the work is, what triggers commission, and what behavior is not allowed.

Clear Rules

Applicants should know the sales track, expected outreach behavior, and what can disqualify activity before they start.

Clean Outreach

Messaging must be accurate, professional, respectful, and aligned with each campaign's approved instructions.

Focused Growth

New roles are opened only when application review, instructions, and handoff processes are ready.
