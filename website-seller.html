Responsibilities

What you will do

  • Find businesses that do not have a website or have an outdated one.
  • Start a professional conversation and confirm basic interest.
  • Submit qualified prospects with clean notes through the approved process.
  • Stay honest about what the build team can provide and when.

Good Fit

Who this works for

  • People who can message local businesses respectfully.
  • Applicants with basic sales confidence or willingness to learn.
  • Anyone who can keep notes and follow up without being pushy.
  • Self-managed workers who understand commissions are paid after close.

Commission

Commission is paid after a qualified website sale closes.

The lead must become a real paying website customer. Duplicate leads, fake buyers, or prospects who never close are not payable.
