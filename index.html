How It Works

A short process, built for clear expectations.

1. Join WhatsApp

Join the public WhatsApp channel/group first so you can receive role updates and instructions.

2. Fill out information

Submit your name, WhatsApp number, email, location, experience, and availability. PSE reviews your fit and assigns the best sales field internally.

3. Wait for instructions

The team reviews your information and shares campaign rules before you begin outreach.

Public Standards

Simple rules before anyone starts.

We want the public site to be clear without publishing private operating details, private customer information, or team metrics.

Allowed

Honest outreach, clear messaging, real prospects, and approved tracking links.

Rejected

Spam, bots, fake traffic, misleading financial claims, copied screenshots, or harassment.

Required

Applicants must follow role instructions and accept that this is commission-based contractor work.
